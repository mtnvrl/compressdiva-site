Compress Diva Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Compress Photo & Video Diva (“Compress Diva”) works on your iPhone. Your photos and videos never leave your device. The only information that leaves it is what's needed to manage your purchase, as explained below.

What we collect

Compress Diva has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never collect your name, email, contacts, location or the photos and videos you work with, and we never sell any information.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

Compressing, trimming, splitting and converting happen on your iPhone. Files you keep in the app's Organizer are stored only in the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete All Data, deletes these files.

Photos access

You pick the exact photos and videos to compress in Apple's photo picker; Compress Diva only receives the items you pick. When you choose Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add items to your library. When you choose Replace Originals, it asks for access to your library so it can delete the originals you replaced; iOS asks you to confirm every delete, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Date and location in your files

By default, the capture date and location already inside a photo or video are kept in the compressed copy. You can turn this off before compressing to remove them. This information stays in the file on your device.

Purchases

Apple handles payment through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. To check whether Compress Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple Account)
  • your purchase history (product, price, dates, renewals, trial status)
  • basic technical data: device model, iOS version, app version, country and language, and your IP address

This information is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and keep subscriptions working. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To ask us to delete the purchase data linked to your anonymous ID, email us and include the date of your purchase so we can find the record.

Children

Compress Diva is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
