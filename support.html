Compress Diva Support

How do I compress?

Tap Choose from Photos or Browse Files, pick your photos or videos, choose a quality or a target size, then tap Compress.

Compress straight from Photos

In the Photos app, select photos or videos, tap Share, then choose Compress Diva. Open Compress Diva and they're waiting on the compress screen. Don't see it? Scroll the app row to the end, tap More and add Compress Diva to Favorites.

Why is a file not smaller?

Some files are already well compressed. Try Smallest, a lower resolution or a target size.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Compress Pro. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.
